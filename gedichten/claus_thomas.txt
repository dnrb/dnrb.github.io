Hugo Claus

On Thomas' Fourth Birthday

Later, my boy, you'll become a man,
Later, you'll yearn like a giraffe to the how and the what for.
They will mark you as luggage.
They will hurt you for your wish and your dream.
And you will try to, once and for all, photograph
the how and what for of the woman
  who turns in your sheets
  who sings as you duplicate in her skin
And even later, little boy, your life
becomes a scrapbook.
But not yet at all, no -- not yet at all.
